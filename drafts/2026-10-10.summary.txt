Today's draft: The NDIS Roster Looks Full. The Travel Between Visits Is Where the Margin Goes. (Category: Industry Focus)

Today is a Saturday, so Industry Focus is the mandated category, and it is also World Mental Health Day, which points to the allied health, psychology and NDIS cluster. The two most obvious angles in that cluster were ruled out as too recent: psychology practice economics was the subject of the 10 September post (Medicare session caps and clinician capacity), and NDIS pricing was covered twice in June through the EOFY wage-versus-price-guide gap and participant plan-renewal timing. This piece opens with the same awareness-day tie-in but moves to a genuinely unclaimed angle for the cluster, the margin that disappears into unbilled travel time between community-based support visits, which none of the earlier NDIS or allied health posts have addressed.
